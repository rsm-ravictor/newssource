Source reliability → reading order and citation

Tier does two jobs. Reading order: it is the third sort key, never the first — it only orders findings that already share a priority and, on the {{ tabs[0].entity_noun }} side, a roster rank. Severity and rank stay the rubric's axes. Citation: a finding is only sent if it can be cited at or above the line below. Weaker sources are still searched and still used for discovery — they just cannot be the link the briefing hands you, because a post is not the record of an event. Where several outlets carry one event, the best-tiered one is cited automatically.
